
# RACE SEASON

[ ] GOAL: become champion by winning the most points over a season

Over [n] race weekends, a player must gather the most Championship Points [CP] to win the championship.
During a race weekend, [$], [XP] and [CP] may be earned.

- [CP] **unlock** available abilities/perks/upgrades.
- [$] / [XP] are required to **purchase** these  upgrades.
- Upgrades may increase maxSpeed, maxSteering (and maxAccel?), increasing the chances of gaining [CP] and becoming champion

# RACE WEEKEND STRUCTURE

- [x] 1. Spawn outside venue 
  - [ ] - A: check in at Parking/entry
  - [ ] - B: park truck in Paddock

- [ ] 2. Setup
  - [ ] A. attend driver meeting @ race control
  - [ ] B. gather supplies (tires, fuel)
  - [ ] C. (optional) equip upgrades
  - [ ] D. Park in garage box

- [ ] 3. Free practice
  - [ ] (optional) car setup
  - [ ] Start practice clock, 10 mins to drive laps
  - [ ] Complete practice programme (set N laps, stay close to racing line)
  - [ ] Return to garage (trailer?) to end session / proceed to Qualifying

- [ ] 4. Qualifying
  - [ ] A. Earn XP for best lap time
  - [ ] B. Earn XP for hitting racing line waypoints
  - [ ] C. Visit scrutineering after qualifying time ends -> skipping may result in random penalty awarded after race

- [ ] 5. Race
  - [ ] Spawn in garage, drive to starting grid slot (simpler: spawn at grid slot)
  - [ ] Drive ~5 laps after starting signal
  - [ ] Earn CP, $ for finising position
  - [ ] Earn XP for # of completed laps
